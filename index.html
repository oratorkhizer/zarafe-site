Why Zarafé

Five things we get right, every event

  1. Built for medical events

    We know the rhythm of a CME: a 20-minute break is 20 minutes. Counters are staffed and open before the session ends.

  2. Menus a doctor would approve

    Portion-controlled plates, low-sugar and sugar-free desserts, high-protein breakfast options, and always a clearly marked diabetic-friendly choice.

  3. Hyderabadi hospitality, done properly

    Dum biryani cooked fresh for the event by our chef team, along with North Indian, South Indian and continental spreads. All non-vegetarian dishes are halal.

  4. Documentation your sponsor needs

    Per-head pricing, itemised bills and headcount-linked invoicing for pharma-sponsored programmes.

  5. One accountable contact

    From the first call to the last plate, you deal with one person, Abdur Raheem, who was at the tasting and will be at the venue.
